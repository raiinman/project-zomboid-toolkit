## Direction contract

The app remains a recognizable native mod-development tool: Setup, Mods and
In-game tests tabs; conventional buttons, forms, tables and an editor. The visual
world contributes type, palette, density and one signature containment header.
It never changes action names or invents fictional telemetry.

- Blackened blue-green shell, oxidized green panels, pale ivory reading text,
  acid-green selection/actions and restrained dried-crimson warning surfaces.
- Actual Dead Signal Interactive logo, with user-approved scratched/grimed
  surfaces while preserving its DS shape and wordmark.
- Original abandoned-facility art stays in the header, away from working controls.
  It must be empty of actual aliens, predators, zombies, figures and creature
  silhouettes. Horror comes from environmental damage, grime and lighting.
- Bundled Oxanium SemiBold for short display headings. Segoe UI body copy and
  Consolas code preserve native readability and familiar data editing.
- Errors and unknown test outcomes keep literal recovery text and distinct
  colors. Never imply a test passed through decorative green lighting alone.
- No flicker, strobe, fake enemy counters, fake connectivity or intrusive motion.
  Progress animation runs only during an actual background tool call.

## Concept assessment

Seven relevant sources were containment glass, quarantined field packaging,
abandoned research signage, outbreak-damaged industrial identification,
scarred creature-hunter armor, night-vision field photography and forensic
specimen documentation. The user's explicit horror brief pins the world.
Impeccable seed 87865f7e assigned the fourth source, translated into the damaged
identification header while the native navigation remains intact.

The barcode challenger was declined because inversion/strobing harms sustained
code reading; its disciplined contrast raises the reading surfaces. The acetate
manual challenger was declined as a visual identity but raises the tab hierarchy
and separation of content layers. Dance notation was declined because it does
not clarify mod or scenario state; its precise grouping raises action alignment.
The pinned user direction overrides conflicting catalog materials.
